# Privacy

DealScope stores search intent, collected offers, area and preferences in browser localStorage. No account or full delivery address is required. At most 30 comparisons (up to 50 offers each) are kept; records older than 30 days are hidden on read and removed on the next save. History controls delete individual/all records immediately. Browser-local storage is not encrypted by the app. Exported files are under the user's control.

Submitted text/image is sent to Mistral only with explicit per-input consent and enabled operator configuration. Images are resized and re-encoded without metadata and are not persisted. The model provider processes data under its policies. Avoid sensitive images/text. AI is optional.

Server routes transiently process requests. Public seller extraction data may be cached for one hour, at most 100 entries. A randomly salted network-identifier hash is used for one-minute in-memory rate limiting. No product history, raw screenshots or identity is sent to analytics; there is no analytics integration. Hosting providers can maintain their own infrastructure logs. The service worker only caches an offline help page; never APIs, searches, images or prices.

Following a source/search link sends the shopper to an external site with its own privacy policy. Links use `noreferrer`. There are no affiliate links in this release. Deleting local history cannot delete content processed earlier by an independent model/hosting provider.
